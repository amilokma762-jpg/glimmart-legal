# Glimmart: AR Drawing & Sketch — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026

Glimmart helps you draw by tracing a template shown over your phone's camera, and turns your own photos into line drawings. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your photos and drawings stay on your phone.**

## 1. Summary

- Glimmart has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own.
- Your camera picture, photos and drawings are processed on your phone and are **not uploaded** by us.
- The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information as described in section 5. You can control this through the consent choices the app shows you.
- You can delete everything the app stores by deleting items inside the app, or by uninstalling it.

## 2. Who we are

Glimmart is made by an independent developer. You can reach us at **amilokma762@gmail.com**.

## 3. What is stored on your phone

Glimmart stores the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- Sketches you create and save from your photos (My sketches).
- Photos of your finished drawings, and time-lapse videos you create (My Album).
- Your lesson progress, drawing time, streak and statistics.
- Your settings (language, reminder time) and which locked templates you have unlocked.

Nothing in this list is sent to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

## 4. Camera and photos

- **Camera:** Glimmart uses the camera to show a live preview under the template while you draw, to take a photo of your finished drawing, and to record time-lapse frames if you choose to record. The camera picture is used only on your phone.
- **Photos you pick:** When you use Photo to Sketch, you choose a photo with the Android photo picker (the app does not get access to your whole gallery) or take one with your camera app. The photo is converted into a drawing on your phone.
- **Saving and sharing:** A drawing, photo or video leaves the app only when **you** choose to save it to your gallery or share it with another app.
- Glimmart does not ask for permission to read your storage.

## 5. Advertising

Glimmart is free and supported by ads shown through **Google AdMob**. Ads appear as a banner on the home screen, as a full-screen ad after you finish a drawing, and as an optional ad you can watch to unlock a template. Ads are never shown while you are drawing, using the camera, or taking a lesson.

To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This helps them show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and prevent fraud. Depending on where you live, the app first asks for your consent using Google's consent message, and you can change your choices there. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Android settings (usually under Settings, then Google, then Ads).

Learn more:
- How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not control how Google handles this data. Its use is governed by Google's own policies.

## 6. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Glimmart schedules **one local notification per day** at the time you choose, between 8:00 and 21:00. It is created by your phone, not by a server, and it contains no personal information. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn the reminder on.

## 7. Permissions the app may ask for

- **Camera:** to draw with the camera and to take photos.
- **Notifications (optional):** only for the daily reminder.

## 8. What we do not do

We do not create accounts, sell your information, track you across other companies' apps, use your photos to train anything, or upload your photos and drawings.

## 9. Children

Glimmart is intended for a general audience and is **not directed to children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us at the address above.

## 10. Your choices

- **Delete content:** you can delete saved sketches and album items inside the app.
- **Delete everything:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's Android settings.
- **Ads and consent:** change your consent choices when the app offers them, and manage ad personalisation in Android settings.
- **Questions or requests:** write to **amilokma762@gmail.com**.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app adds a feature that handles information differently. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be reflected in the app or its store listing.
